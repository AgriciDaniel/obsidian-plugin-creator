# Session Handoff

## Intent and contract

Record the original user task, latest steering, acceptance criteria, approved
scope, ownership, and external-action boundaries.

## Current state

Record changed files, exact artifact and dependency hashes, commands that ran,
observed results by evidence class, failures, and untouched unrelated work.

## Decisions and dissent

Record selected options, tradeoffs, official source IDs/dates, contradictory
evidence, alternatives that remain plausible, and project-specific assumptions.

## Next action

Name one concrete task, its files, expected output, and verification. Distinguish
work that can proceed locally from an actual permission or missing-input blocker.

## Safe continuation

Read the project's instructions, product brief, architecture, acceptance record,
and source list before acting. Preserve private content and immutable evidence.
Never promote static, retained, or generated results into actual runtime,
physical-device, human-acceptance, or public-release claims.
