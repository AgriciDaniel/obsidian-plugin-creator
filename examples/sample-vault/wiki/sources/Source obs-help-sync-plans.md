# Source obs-help-sync-plans

Original paraphrase of [Obsidian Help: Plans and storage limits (Obsidian Sync)](https://help.obsidian.md/sync/plans); reviewed 2026-11-01.

- As retrieved on 2026-11-01, Sync Standard permits files up to 5 MB and 1 GB storage; Sync Plus permits files up to 200 MB and storage tiers from 10 GB to 100 GB.
- Version history and attachments count toward storage. Reaching the account limit stops file syncing and prompts pruning.
